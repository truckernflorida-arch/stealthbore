How to read it

  • + pitch = climb, getting shallower (Vermeer / DigiTrak convention).
  • − pitch = dive, getting deeper.
  • Percent is the field number: 1% over 100 ft is 1 ft of rise or fall. Degrees are the same slope as an angle.
  • Formula: (current depth − target depth) ÷ remaining distance × 100. This is the straight-shot hold. Soil and rod bend will still move the head.
